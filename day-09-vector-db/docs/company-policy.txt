Travel Policy

Employees travelling within the UK receive £50 per day as a travel allowance.

----------------------------------

Annual Leave Policy

Employees receive 25 days of annual leave every year.

----------------------------------

Remote Working Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days each week with manager approval.

----------------------------------

Training Policy

Every employee receives a £1000 annual learning budget for certifications and technical training.

----------------------------------

Medical Insurance

All permanent employees are covered by the company medical insurance policy.
